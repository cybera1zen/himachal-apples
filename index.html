Where they grow

Six districts,
one mountain crop

01

Shimla

Kotgarh · Kotkhai · Jubbal · Thanedar

The heart of Himachal apple country. In 1916 Samuel Stokes planted American Delicious apples at Kotgarh, and the hills changed for good.

02

Kullu

Beas valley

Orchards run along the Beas under pine forest and snow peaks. A key belt for both old Delicious and newer spur strains.

03

Mandi

Karsog · Janjehli · Gohar · Sundernagar

Karsog is Mandi's biggest apple area, followed by Janjehli, Gohar and Sundernagar.

04

Kinnaur

Kalpa · Sangla · Reckong Peo

Orchards above 10,000 ft. Known for sweetness, colour, crunch and long shelf life. The last apples of the season.

05

Chamba

Western hills

A smaller apple district in the west of the state, counted in Himachal's apple area and output.

06

Lahaul-Spiti

Cold desert

Dry, high and cold. Known for quality apples, with high-density orchards now spreading here.

Harvest

The harvest climbs the hills

Picking starts in the lower orchards and moves up with the season. Kinnaur comes in last, from the end of September into November.

  1. JulLower orchards start. Early Gala types.
  2. AugMid hills in full swing. Shimla belt picking.
  3. SepRoyal Delicious in season. Higher orchards follow.
  4. Oct - NovKinnaur and the highest valleys finish.
