Where they grow

Three valleys, one taste of the hills

01

Shimla

The heart of Himachal apple country. Kotgarh, Thanedar, Jubbal and Kotkhai orchards line the slopes.

02

Kullu

Orchards along the Beas river, framed by pine forest and snow peaks.

03

Kinnaur

High, dry and cold. Apples grown here are known for colour, crunch and long shelf life.

Harvest

Picked July to October

Lower orchards pick first. The highest valleys finish last, when the first snow is close.
